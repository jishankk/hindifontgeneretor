Welcome to HindiFontGen (hindifontgen.in). We provide a free online tool that lets you preview Hindi text in different Devanagari fonts, adjust its appearance, copy text and download images.

Like many websites, HindiFontGen may use cookies or similar technologies for basic website functions and, where applicable, third-party services. This policy explains these technologies in straightforward terms so you can understand what they do and how to manage them.

A quick note about this website: HindiFontGen currently uses your browser's local storage to remember that you have acknowledged its privacy notice. This is different from a traditional cookie. The font generator also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Third-party cookies may be used if advertising or other cookie-enabled services are provided.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store in your browser or on your device. They can help a website remember certain settings, maintain a session, understand how its pages are used or support advertising services.

Similar technologies include browser local storage and other methods that allow information to be stored or accessed on a device. Although they serve related purposes, local storage and cookies work differently.

2. How HindiFontGen uses these technologies

Remembering the privacy notice

HindiFontGen uses your browser's local storage to remember when you acknowledge the privacy notice displayed on the website. This helps prevent the same notice from appearing every time you visit a page.

The preference is stored in your browser under the key hfg_cookie_consent. It is not a traditional cookie, and it is not used by the font generator to save the Hindi text you enter.

Loading fonts and displaying the website

Our website loads typefaces through Google Fonts so that Hindi and other Devanagari text can be displayed in different styles. Your browser connects to Google's servers to request the required font resources. Google may receive technical information associated with those requests, such as your IP address and browser details, in accordance with its own practices.

You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Fonts privacy information .

Website functionality

The font previews, colour controls and image creation features are designed to work in your browser. We do not use the privacy-notice preference to collect or store the text you type into the generator.

3. Third-party services and advertising

Some resources or services available through HindiFontGen may be provided by third parties. These providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies and settings.

For example, Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used by our generator. If advertising or another third-party service is enabled on the website, that service may use additional technologies to provide its functionality, measure performance or prevent abuse.

Third-party technologies are governed by the relevant provider's policies. HindiFontGen does not control every cookie or data processing activity carried out by an external service.

4. Google advertising cookies

HindiFontGen may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners, where those services are enabled.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity, measure advertising performance and, where permitted by the applicable settings and consent requirements, personalise advertisements based on browsing activity.

The technologies used and the information processed may vary depending on the advertising service, your location, your settings and the choices available to you. We do not control the operation of third-party advertising cookies.

For more information about Google's advertising practices, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

You can review or change available Google ad personalisation settings through Google Ads Settings . The settings available to you may depend on your account, location and whether you are signed in.

5. How to manage cookies

Most web browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through their privacy or security settings. The exact steps depend on the browser and device you use.

You can generally manage cookies by following these steps:

  • Open your browser's Settings menu.
  • Find the Privacy, Security or Site Settings section.
  • Open the cookies or site-data controls.
  • Choose whether to allow, block or delete cookies and stored site data.

You can also clear the site data stored for HindiFontGen. Doing so may remove the saved privacy-notice preference, so the notice may appear again on a later visit.

Blocking all cookies or site storage may affect certain website functions. External services may also behave differently when their cookies are blocked. Some advertising technologies may continue to operate differently depending on the service and applicable requirements.

6. Your text and other information

The text you enter into the Hindi Font Generator is processed in your browser for previews, styling and PNG creation. HindiFontGen does not save that entered text to its own server as part of these features.

However, loading external resources, visiting the website or using third-party services may involve the processing of technical information by those providers. For details about how information is handled, please read our Privacy Policy .
