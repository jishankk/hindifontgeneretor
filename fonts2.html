Choosing a Hindi font for poetry, invitations and devotional text

The fonts on this page are mostly traditional and book-style. They are a good fit when your words should feel calm, formal or cultural rather than loud: shayari, poems, wedding and puja invitations, certificates, religious quotes and anything written to be read slowly.

Type your own words in the box above before you decide. A font that looks beautiful on a sample line can look very different with your name, a long verse or a line full of joined letters.

Which font for which occasion

  • Poetry and shayari: Kurale and Halant have a bookish, calm look that suits short verses. Keep the lines short and leave plenty of space around them.
  • Invitations and certificates: Shobhika and Sura look formal. Use a dark colour on a plain light background and avoid heavy effects.
  • Sanskrit shlokas and mantras: Sanskrit Text and Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit are made with Sanskrit in mind. Always check the full verse in the preview before you download, because rare signs are not supported equally well in every font.
  • Marathi text: Tiro Devanagari Marathi uses letter forms tuned for Marathi. The Hindi and Sanskrit versions of the same family look similar, so compare all three with your own text.
  • Short captions and headings: Sarala and Amiko are simpler and work well when the text is only a line or two.

Quick checks before you download

  • Type a word with joined letters, such as श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता or विद्यार्थी, and look at how the conjuncts are drawn.
  • Check that the matras (the signs above and below letters) do not touch or overlap the line above.
  • Use Size to preview your text at the real size you plan to use. Thin traditional fonts can look weak when they are small.
  • Avoid the outline effect on thin or calligraphic fonts. It can eat into the fine strokes and make the text hard to read.
  • Use Transparent if you want to place the text on your own background in a design app.

Using the font outside this page

Copying text from here never copies the font. To use one of these fonts in Word, Canva or another app, open its Google Fonts page from the list above, download the family and install it on your device. The how to use guide explains the steps.
