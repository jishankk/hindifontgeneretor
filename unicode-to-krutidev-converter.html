What is this Unicode to KrutiDev converter?

Standard Unicode Devanagari (Mangal) text can be converted into Kruti Dev 010 and Kruti Dev 055 legacy font encoding using our free online unicode to krutidev converter. Kruti Dev is the standard font used in court documents, printing presses, DTP design, and government typing exams (CPCT, SSC, State PSCs) throughout India.

Unicode encoding is used to create the text that appears when you type in Hindi on smartphones, WhatsApp, or Google Input Tools. Nonetheless, the Kruti Dev font is still needed by numerous Government of India offices, printing presses, DTP software (like CorelDraw and PageMaker), and typing exams (like CPCT, High Court, and SSC). By instantly remapping character codes, this tool closes this gap and ensures that your Hindi text appears correctly in offline design tools.
