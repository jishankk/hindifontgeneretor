How the typing rules work

This tool follows how a word sounds, and you decide the vowel length. A short a stays silent inside a word (kal becomes कल), and a long vowel needs two letters (kaal becomes काल). That one habit fixes most spelling surprises.

Half letters. Two consonants typed together usually join only where Hindi really joins them, as in dost (दोस्त), kya (क्या) and bachche (बच्चे). In other places the tool keeps the short a, so karna gives करना and sakte gives सकते. If it guesses wrong, type an a to split two letters or a + to join them.

The letter n before k, g, ch, j, t, d, p, b or s turns into the dot ं, so hindi becomes हिंदी and sundar becomes सुंदर. A short list of very common words (है, हैं, मैं, नहीं, और, कुछ and similar) is built in, because their spelling does not follow the sound.

Questions people ask

Is this a translator?

No. It does not translate meaning. It writes the sound of your Roman letters in Devanagari, so pyaar becomes प्यार, while the English word love becomes लव and not प्यार. For English words, type them the way Hindi speakers say them, such as time for टाइम.

Why is my word spelled slightly wrong?

Hindi vowel length cannot be guessed from Roman spelling alone, because kitab could be a few different words. Type kitaab for किताब and bhaarat for भारत. Use a to keep two consonants apart and + to join them.

Will the font stay when I paste the text into another app?

No. Copy gives plain Unicode text, and the app you paste into shows it in its own font. To keep a style, download the PNG, or install the font from its Google Fonts page. The Hindi font generator lists all 61 fonts it supports.

Is my text stored or sent anywhere?

The conversion and the PNG are made inside your browser, and the text is not saved on a server. Your browser does load the fonts from Google Fonts. The Privacy Policy explains this in full.

Does it work for Marathi, Nepali or Sanskrit?

The rules are tuned for Hindi. Many Marathi, Nepali and Sanskrit words come out correctly because they use the same script, but letters such as ळ are not included.

I need Kruti Dev or Shree Lipi text. What do I do?

This tool produces Unicode. Paste the result into the Unicode to Kruti Dev converter or the Unicode to Shree Lipi converter to change it to the older font-based format.
