Pairing a bold headline font with a readable serif

This page has two groups: bold fonts (Baloo 2, Pragati Narrow, Khula and Kadwa) and classic serif fonts (Eczar, Noto Serif Devanagari, Inknut Antiqua, Vesper Libre, Rhodium Libre and Glegoo). They work best as a pair: a bold font for the headline and a calmer serif for the text below it.

A simple pairing method

  • Headline: Baloo 2 or Khula for a modern, friendly look. Kadwa or Eczar for a traditional look.
  • Body text: Noto Serif Devanagari or Vesper Libre stay comfortable in longer text.
  • Rule of thumb: use only two fonts in one design. One should be bold and simple, the other calm and readable.
  • Avoid: two decorative fonts together. Inknut Antiqua and Rhodium Libre have strong character, so use one of them at most.

Spacing tips that matter for Devanagari

  • Hindi letters carry matras above and below the line, so they need more room between lines than English. A line height of about 1.5 to 1.8 is a good starting point for body text.
  • Letters in Devanagari hang from a top line (the shirorekha). Keep the text aligned so that line stays clear and unbroken.
  • Bold fonts need more space between lines at large sizes, otherwise the matras of one line touch the next.
  • Do not use all-bold text for paragraphs. Keep bold for headings and short highlights.
