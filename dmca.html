Copyright & DMCA Policy

HindiFontGen respects intellectual property rights and provides a process for reporting material that may infringe copyright.

Copyright ownership

Unless otherwise stated, the original website design, written content, branding, and code created for HindiFontGen belong to their respective rights holders. Third-party fonts and resources remain subject to their own licenses and rights.

Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe that material available through HindiFontGen infringes your copyright, please send a notice to contact@hindifontgen.in.

Your notice should include enough information for us to understand the claim, including:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The specific URL or location of the material in question.
  • A brief explanation of why you believe the material infringes your rights.
  • A statement that the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge and that you are authorized to make the complaint.

Review of notices

We may review copyright complaints and take appropriate action where a claim appears valid and actionable. This may include removing or restricting access to material where appropriate.

False or abusive notices

Please do not submit knowingly false, misleading, or abusive copyright complaints. Such notices may be disregarded or handled as appropriate.

Third-party fonts

HindiFontGen uses third-party fonts and resources. Copyright and licensing rights for those resources belong to their respective creators or providers. If your concern relates specifically to a font, please identify the font and the relevant material in your message.
