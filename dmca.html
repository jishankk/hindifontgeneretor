HindiFontGen respects the intellectual property rights of creators, publishers, font designers and other rights holders. We take copyright concerns seriously and encourage anyone who believes that material available on our website infringes their rights to contact us with clear and relevant details.

This policy describes how to submit a copyright complaint concerning hindifontgen.in. It is intended to help us identify and review reported material. It does not guarantee that every request will result in removal.

1. Purpose of This Policy

This DMCA Policy provides a process for reporting content that you believe infringes copyright. We review notices in good faith and may take appropriate action when a complaint is sufficiently clear and supported by the information provided.

The Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) is a United States copyright law. Where applicable, notices and counter-notices may be subject to its requirements. Other copyright laws may apply depending on the circumstances and the location of the parties.

2. Content on HindiFontGen

HindiFontGen provides browser-based tools for previewing Hindi and other Devanagari text in different fonts. Font families may be delivered by third-party providers, and their respective creators or licensors may retain ownership of those fonts.

The presence of a font, link, description or other material on this website does not necessarily mean that HindiFontGen owns that material. If you believe that content displayed on a page under our control infringes your copyright, please identify the specific material and its location.

If your concern relates to a font or service hosted and controlled by a third party, you may also need to contact that provider directly.

3. How to Submit a Copyright Notice

If you are a copyright owner or authorised to act on behalf of one, you can send a copyright complaint to:

Copyright Contact

Email: contact@hindifontgen.in

Suggested subject: Copyright Complaint – HindiFontGen

To help us understand and assess your request, please include the following information where applicable:

  1. Your full name and contact details, including an email address where we can reach you.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
  3. The exact URL of the page or material on HindiFontGen that you want us to review.
  4. An explanation of why you believe the identified material infringes your copyright or the rights of the person you represent.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, where legally required, that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf.
  7. Your physical or electronic signature, where required for a formal notice.

Please provide enough information for us to identify the specific material and understand your concern. Avoid sending passwords, payment information or unrelated sensitive personal information.

4. How We Review Copyright Notices

After receiving a complaint, we may review the information provided and determine whether further clarification is needed. We may contact the reporting person for additional details if the notice does not clearly identify the material or the rights involved.

Where appropriate and legally justified, we may remove or restrict access to material under our control. We may also decline to act on a notice that is incomplete, unclear or does not provide a reasonable basis for the requested action.

We aim to handle copyright concerns responsibly, but we do not guarantee a specific response time or outcome. Please do not assume that material has been removed until you receive confirmation or verify the result.

5. Counter-Notice

If material you provided or have rights to use has been removed or restricted following a copyright complaint and you believe this action was mistaken, you may contact us at the email address above with an explanation and the relevant URL.

Include your contact details, identify the affected material and explain why you believe the removal or restriction was incorrect. If you are submitting a formal counter-notice under the DMCA, additional statements and information may be legally required.

A message sent to HindiFontGen is not automatically a legally valid DMCA counter-notice. Where a formal statutory process applies, the requirements of the relevant law must be followed. We will review counter-notices and determine any appropriate next steps.

6. Repeat Infringement

Where appropriate and supported by the circumstances, we may take action against repeated copyright infringement involving material or activity under our control. Any action will depend on the nature of the issue, the information available and applicable law.

7. Good-Faith Reporting

Please make sure that you have a genuine basis for submitting a copyright complaint. Before sending a notice, consider whether the material may be licensed, used with permission or otherwise legally permitted.

Knowingly submitting materially false or misleading copyright claims may have legal consequences. If you are unsure whether a use infringes copyright, consider obtaining qualified legal advice before submitting a formal notice.

8. Third-Party Content and Services

Some fonts and technical features available through HindiFontGen may rely on third-party services. We may not host or control every font file, external page or linked resource displayed or referenced by the website.

If your complaint concerns material hosted by another provider, please identify the exact resource in your message. You may also need to contact the provider that hosts or controls that material. We cannot guarantee that a third party will respond to or act on a complaint.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this DMCA Policy when our website, processes or applicable requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page, along with an updated effective date where appropriate.

We encourage visitors and rights holders to review this page periodically.
