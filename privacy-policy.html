Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how HindiFontGen handles information when you visit and use this website.

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. Review the privacy policy of any third-party service you visit.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website secure, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit confidential information through public website forms or email unless specifically required.
