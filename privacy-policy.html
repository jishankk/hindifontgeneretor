Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HindiFontGen.in ("we", "us", "our website") handles information when you visit and use our Hindi Font Generator tool. We built this site to be simple and privacy-respecting, and this page sets out exactly what happens to your data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The core function of this website — typing text and previewing it in different Hindi fonts — runs entirely inside your own browser. The text you type into the tool is never sent to our servers, stored in any database, or shared with anyone. When you close the tab or refresh the page, that text is gone.

We do not require account creation, sign-up, or login to use any feature of this site.

2. Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our server and any third-party services we use (such as analytics or advertising providers) may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/region level)
  • Referring website or search terms that led you here

This information is used only in aggregate form to understand how visitors use the site and to improve performance. It is not used to personally identify individual visitors by us.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. This site may use cookies for:

  • Essential functionality — remembering basic preferences such as your last-selected settings.
  • Analytics — understanding aggregate traffic patterns (for example, via Google Analytics).
  • Advertising — third-party advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the site or ads display, but it will not affect the core font-preview tool.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We may display advertisements on this website through Google AdSense and/or other third-party ad networks. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services that may interest you.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendor cookies by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.
  • Third-party ad vendors' use of cookies is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. We do not control the content or cookies used by these third parties.

5. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to help us understand how visitors interact with the site. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyse usage data. The information collected is generally aggregated and does not directly identify you.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site includes links to external sites, such as Google Fonts specimen pages. We are not responsible for the content, privacy practices, or terms of any third-party websites you may visit through these links. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Data Security

Since the tool itself processes your text locally in your browser and does not transmit or store it on our servers, there is minimal personal data for us to secure in relation to the core tool. Where any data is collected (such as through analytics or advertising cookies), we rely on the security practices of the respective third-party providers.

9. Your Choices

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser at any time. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, though this may limit certain features. You may also use browser extensions or opt-out tools to limit ad personalisation.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us at contact@hindifontgen.in.
