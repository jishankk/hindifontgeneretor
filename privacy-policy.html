Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how HindiFontGen handles information when you visit and use the website.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

HindiFontGen (“we”, “us”, “our”, or “the website”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit hindifontgen.in, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information You Enter Into the Tool

The Hindi Font Generator is designed to process the text you enter in your browser. The font preview and PNG generation features operate on your device. HindiFontGen does not require you to create an account or submit your text to use the core tool.

Text entered into the font generator is intended to be processed locally in your browser. Please do not enter information into any public website that you would not want to process or display on your own device.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, certain technical information may be made available through your browser, hosting provider, security systems, analytics services, or advertising services. This can include IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited, approximate usage information, and timestamps.

Such information may be used for website operation, security, performance measurement, troubleshooting, analytics, and improving the site.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

HindiFontGen may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary for website functionality, analytics, security, or advertising. Cookies are small files or pieces of data that may be stored by your browser.

If advertising is displayed, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertisements in accordance with their policies and applicable settings.

5. Google Advertising Services

HindiFontGen may use Google advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this and other websites.

Visitors may have choices regarding personalized advertising through Google's available advertising and privacy controls. Information about Google's data practices is governed by Google's own policies and settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party services for web fonts, analytics, advertising, hosting, security, or external font information. These third parties may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

For example, the website loads fonts from Google Fonts and may link to Google Fonts pages when a visitor chooses to view a typeface.

7. External Links

HindiFontGen may contain links to external websites. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices or content. Please review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and reliability of the website. However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

HindiFontGen is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at collecting personal information from children. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, its features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hindifontgen@gmail.com.
