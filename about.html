About Us

Making Hindi typography easy for everyone

HindiFontGen.in was built with one simple goal: make it easy for anyone to see their Hindi text in a variety of genuine, well-designed fonts — instantly, for free, and without needing any design software.

Whether you're writing an Instagram caption, designing a wedding invitation, putting together a YouTube thumbnail, or just want your WhatsApp status to look a little more expressive, finding the right Devanagari font used to mean digging through scattered font websites, downloading files, and testing them one by one in an editor. We wanted a faster way — type once, and see your exact text rendered across dozens of fonts side by side, in real time.

What We Offer

Our core tool lets you type text in Hindi (or English, which most fonts here also render cleanly) and instantly preview it in 50+ genuine, openly licensed Hindi/Devanagari fonts sourced from Google Fonts. You can adjust the font size, change the text and background colours, copy the plain text, or download your styled text as a PNG image — including with a transparent background for easy use in other designs.

Why We Built It This Way

We chose to keep everything running directly in your browser rather than on a server. That means:

  • Your text is never uploaded, logged, or stored anywhere — it stays on your device.
  • Previews update instantly as you type, with no loading delays.
  • There's no account, sign-up, or personal information required to use the tool.

We also made a deliberate choice to only use real, Unicode-based Devanagari fonts rather than character-substitution tricks. This means the text you generate stays accessible, searchable, and correctly readable across platforms — not just a visual approximation.

Who This Is For

Students, content creators, small business owners designing their own marketing material, event planners making invitation cards, teachers preparing classroom materials, and anyone who simply enjoys good typography — this tool is built for all of you.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep this tool free, fast, and genuinely useful. We continue to test and add new fonts, refine the interface based on how people actually use it, and keep the site light enough to work well even on slower connections and older devices.

If you have suggestions, spot an issue, or want to request a font style, we'd love to hear from you — see our Contact Us page for details.
