What is a Hindi font generator?

A Hindi font generator shows the same Unicode Hindi text in many different Devanagari typefaces side by side. Instead of switching fonts one by one in a design app, you type once and compare every style at the same time.

Nothing is converted into symbols. Your text stays normal Unicode, and only the typeface changes how it looks. That is why this tool is useful for names, quotes, Instagram and WhatsApp graphics, wedding cards, posters and thumbnails: you see your real words, with real matras and conjuncts, before you design anything.

Pick a style, adjust size and colours, then use Copy for plain text or PNG and Transparent to save the exact styled look as an image.

Useful knowledge

Unicode Hindi fonts vs. old font-based text

Modern Hindi typing uses Unicode Devanagari. A word like नमस्ते is stored as standard characters that can be searched, copied and read by any website or app. The font only changes how those characters look.

Older legacy workflows (such as KrutiDev) depend on a special character mapping, so changing the font alone does not convert them. If you have legacy text, use the Unicode ⇄ KrutiDev converter. To find a specific symbol or matra, try the Hindi character map.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best Hindi font?

It depends on the job. For readable text, clean fonts like Hind, Mukta or Noto Sans Devanagari work well. For names, quotes and greeting cards, handwritten styles such as Kalam, Sarala or Halant look distinctive. For wedding invitations, Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Eczar or Rozha One look elegant. For posters and thumbnails, try bold styles like Yatra One, Modak or Khand.

Will copied text keep its font style?

No. The clipboard only stores plain Unicode text, so the app you paste into shows it in its own font. Download the PNG if you need the exact look.

Can I use these fonts for commercial work?

All fonts in this tool come from Google Fonts, which are published under open licences (mostly the SIL Open Font License). For a large commercial project, check the licence on the font's Google Fonts page first.

Does it work for Marathi, Sanskrit or Nepali?

Yes. Any text written in Devanagari script works, including Marathi, Sanskrit, Nepali and Bhojpuri.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Previews and PNG creation happen in your browser, and the text you type is not saved by this tool. Font files are loaded from Google Fonts. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I use a font in Word?

Click the Font button on a card to open its Google Fonts page, download the family and install it on your computer. It will then appear in Word's font list.

Is this the same as a "fancy text" generator?

No. Fancy text generators swap letters for special Unicode symbols, which mostly work for English letters and not for Devanagari. HindiFontGen keeps your normal Hindi characters and applies a real font, so the text stays searchable. To keep the look outside this tool, download the PNG.

Why do some letters look different or broken in a font?

Matras and conjuncts are drawn differently in every typeface, so a full sentence shows real readability better than a single word. If a style looks odd for your phrase, open its Google Fonts page with the Font button to check the glyphs, or simply pick another style.
