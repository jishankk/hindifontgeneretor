What is a Hindi font generator?

Typography basics

What makes Hindi text look right in a font

Most Devanagari letters hang from a horizontal line called the shirorekha. In a well-made font that line runs unbroken from one letter to the next, so a word reads as a single strip. If you see small gaps in it, the cause is usually extra letter-spacing added in a design app. Leave letter-spacing at zero for Hindi.

Matras (vowel signs) sit above, below or beside the letter, as in कि, की, कु, कू, के and कै. They need vertical room, so a tight line height can make them touch the line above. For text on more than one line, about 1.5 times the font size is a safe line height.

Conjuncts such as क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ and श्र are single shapes built from two or more letters. Most modern fonts draw them well, but some decorative fonts stack them awkwardly or show a visible halant (्). That is the main reason to test your own phrase instead of judging a font by one letter.

Thin strokes also do not survive shrinking. Small marks like the chandrabindu (ँ) and the nukta (the dot in ड़ or क़) are the first to vanish, so pick a medium or bold weight for anything people will read on a phone.

Useful knowledge

Unicode Hindi fonts vs. old font-based text

Modern Hindi typing uses Unicode Devanagari. A word like नमस्ते is stored as standard characters that can be searched, copied and read by any website or app. The font only changes how those characters look.

Older legacy workflows (such as KrutiDev) depend on a special character mapping, so changing the font alone does not convert them. If you have legacy text, use the Unicode ⇄ KrutiDev converter. To find a specific symbol or matra, try the Hindi character map.

Using your PNG on social media and in designs

  • Canva or Photoshop: download the Transparent PNG and drop it onto any photo or background.
  • Instagram and WhatsApp: download the normal PNG with your chosen background colour and post it as an image or status.
  • Word or other apps: pasted text uses the app's own font. Download the font from its Google Fonts page and install it, or use the PNG.

Which effect works on which background

  • Outline keeps a busy photo visible through the letters. Use it with bold fonts, because a thin font looks weak when only the edges are drawn.
  • Shadow separates text from a light photo or plain light background without changing the colour.
  • Glow needs a dark background. On white it is almost invisible.
  • 3D suits short headings of a word or two. On a long line it turns messy.
  • Gradient blends from your text colour to purple. Keep it to a headline or one short line.

Image sizes and PNG output

The PNG you download is drawn at three times the size shown in the preview, so a 40px preview gives letters about 120px tall. That is plenty for a sticker, caption or overlay. For a full post, download the Transparent PNG and place it at the canvas size you need in your design app.

  • Instagram square post: 1080 × 1080 px
  • Instagram story, reel or WhatsApp status: 1080 × 1920 px
  • YouTube thumbnail: 1280 × 720 px

As a rough guide, a headline on a 1080px-wide post needs letters around 80 to 150px tall to stay readable on a phone.

Common problems and quick fixes

  • Boxes instead of letters: the font is still loading or does not contain that character. Wait a second, then try another style.
  • Different look after pasting into another app: the clipboard carries text only, never the font. Use the PNG when the look must stay exactly the same.
  • Letters clipped at the top in your design app: matras need space. Increase the line height instead of squeezing lines together.
  • A dot floating beside ड़, ढ़ or क़: some keyboards type the nukta as a separate character and not every font joins it neatly. Retype the letter or try a different font.
  • PNG spacing differs slightly from the preview: the image is drawn on a canvas, so tiny spacing differences can appear. Open the PNG once before you publish it.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best Hindi font?

It depends on the job. For readable text, clean fonts like Hind, Mukta or Noto Sans Devanagari work well. For wedding cards and invitations, Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Eczar or Rozha One look elegant. For posters and thumbnails, try bold styles like Yatra One, Modak or Khand.

Will copied text keep its font style?

No. The clipboard only stores plain Unicode text, so the app you paste into shows it in its own font. Download the PNG if you need the exact look.

Can I use these fonts for commercial work?

All fonts in this tool come from Google Fonts, which are published under open licences (mostly the SIL Open Font License). For a large commercial project, check the licence on the font's Google Fonts page first.

Does it work for Marathi, Sanskrit or Nepali?

Yes. Any text written in Devanagari script works, including Marathi, Sanskrit, Nepali and Bhojpuri.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Previews and PNG creation happen in your browser, and the text you type is not saved by this tool.

How do I use a font in Word?

Click the Font button on a card to open its Google Fonts page, download the family and install it on your computer. It will then appear in Word's font list.

Which font is best for names?

Slightly bold or handwritten styles such as Sarala, Halant or Kalam usually make names look distinctive.

How do I type Hindi if I only have an English keyboard?

On a phone, add Hindi in your keyboard settings. Gboard and the iPhone keyboard both offer a transliteration mode where typing "namaste" gives नमस्ते. On Windows or Mac, add Hindi as an input language in the system settings. You can also copy Hindi text from any website and paste it into the box above.

What size is the PNG I download?

It is three times the size of the preview, with a small margin around the text. Choose Transparent if you plan to resize it or place it on a photo in another app.

Why do the previews take a moment to appear?

Fonts are loaded only for the cards on the current page, and only for the letters you typed, which keeps the download small. On a slow connection a card may show a plain font for a second before the real style appears.

Why is the text limited to 200 characters?

The limit keeps previews fast, because every card redraws as you type. For a long paragraph, use the tool to choose a font and then set the text in your design app.

Is this the same as a "fancy text" generator?

No. Fancy text generators swap letters for special Unicode symbols, which mostly work for English letters and not for Devanagari. HindiFontGen keeps your normal Hindi characters and applies a real font, so the text stays searchable. To keep the look outside this tool, download the PNG.

Why do some letters look different or broken in a font?

Matras and conjuncts are drawn differently in every typeface. If a style looks odd for your phrase, open its Google Fonts page with the Font button to check the glyphs, or simply pick another style.

Why test a full sentence instead of one word?

Devanagari uses combining marks and conjuncts whose spacing and shape differ by typeface, so a full phrase shows real readability.
