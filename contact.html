Get in Touch

Contact Us

Have a question about HindiFontGen, found something that isn't working correctly, or want to suggest a font? Send us an email. Your feedback helps us improve the website and make the font tools more useful.

Send Us an Email

For general questions, technical problems, suggestions, or other enquiries, email us at the address below.

contact@hindifontgen.in

We aim to reply as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the type and number of enquiries we receive.

How Can We Help?

You can contact us about topics such as:

  • Reporting a broken feature, display issue, or other technical problem
  • Suggesting a Hindi or Devanagari font style for the website
  • Sharing feedback or ideas for improving the font tools
  • Asking questions about using the website or creating font-style images
  • Questions about our Privacy Policy or Terms & Conditions
  • Advertising, business, or partnership enquiries

Before You Contact Us

If you're reporting a technical issue, please explain what you were trying to do and what happened instead. Mentioning the browser or device you're using may also help us understand the problem.

Please don't include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information in your message.

A quick note: HindiFontGen currently handles enquiries by email. We don't offer phone or live-chat support. Email allows us to review each message and respond when we can.
