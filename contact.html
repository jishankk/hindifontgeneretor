Contact Us

Have a question, found a problem, or want to suggest a Hindi font? Get in touch with us by email. Your feedback helps us make HindiFontGen more useful.

Get in Touch

HindiFontGen offers tools for previewing Hindi text in different Devanagari font styles. If you have a question about using the website, experience a technical issue, or have an idea for improving the tools, you can contact us directly.

Email Support

For general questions, technical problems, suggestions, feedback, or business enquiries, email us at:

contact@hindifontgen.in

We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the nature and number of messages we receive.

What Can You Contact Us About?

  • Reporting a broken feature, error, or display problem.
  • Suggesting a Hindi or Devanagari font style.
  • Sharing feedback about the website or its tools.
  • Asking how to use the font generator or save a styled PNG image.
  • Questions about our Privacy Policy or Terms & Conditions.
  • Advertising, business, or partnership enquiries.

Reporting a Technical Issue

Please explain what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected to happen instead. If relevant, include the browser and device you are using. This information can help us understand the problem.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information in your email.

About the Author

Ali Hamza

Ali Hamza is the creator of HindiFontGen, a website built to help people preview Hindi text in different Devanagari fonts and create styled text images. Feedback and suggestions from visitors help guide improvements to the website.

For questions or feedback, email contact@hindifontgen.in.
