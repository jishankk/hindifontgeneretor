Get in Touch

Contact Us

We're a small team behind HindiFontGen.in, and we're always happy to hear from the people who use the tool. Whether you've found a bug, have a font request, want to give feedback, or just have a general question, drop us a message.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email at contact@hindifontgen.in. We read every message that comes in and try to respond within a couple of business days.

What You Can Write to Us About

  • Reporting a bug or something that isn't working as expected
  • Requesting a new font style to be added
  • General feedback or suggestions to improve the tool
  • Questions about our Privacy Policy or Terms & Conditions
  • Advertising or partnership enquiries

We currently don't offer phone or live chat support, but email works well for us and lets us give every message proper attention.
